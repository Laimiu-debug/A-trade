A-trade's read-only TQ connection and MCP isolation design is adapted from
TDX-local-MCP and the local TRADE implementation:
https://github.com/adambbhe/TDX-local-MCP
Pinned upstream source commit: f0bef2e996f92e08beaffb5fb14968a65ef10a2a

Only local initialization, status and market-data read operations are included.
The upstream alert engine, notifier and trading tools are not included.
The vendor's tqcenter.py and TPythClient.dll are not distributed by A-trade.
